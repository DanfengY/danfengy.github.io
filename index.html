I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the Toulouse School of Economics. My research focuses on the economics of knowledge and innovation.

I study how firms create, combine, and use knowledge to innovate, adapt to changes in their economic environment, and generate value.

My research interests include innovation economics, technological change, intellectual property, and the organization of innovation.
